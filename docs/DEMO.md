1. **0:00 — Problem.** “A dub can sound natural while changing the lesson. This prototype helps a bilingual creator review possible changes with evidence.” Show the original 24 participants and target 48.
2. **0:20 — Human decision.** Choose Needs correction, replace 48 with 24 in the script, add a reviewer/reason, and save. Point out that original evidence remains visible.
3. **0:45 — Avoid a trivial false alarm.** Open 00:28: 60 seconds versus one minute. Supported unit conversion prevents a quantity flag, but the passage is still unreviewed.
4. **1:00 — Other risk types.** Open 00:14 for possible negation loss and 00:21 for a creator glossary mismatch. Say “possible”; do not imply native-language validation.
5. **1:20 — Real workflow.** Open the local media section. Explain that timestamps can seek creator-owned files and that media is never uploaded. The sample includes no audio, so do not pretend to play it.
6. **1:35 — Handoff.** Export SRT and the Markdown report. Explain why the file is a draft and why corrected captions are not corrected audio.
7. **1:50 — Product judgment.** “There are 36 authored evaluation cases, including known misses and false flags. The next validation is independent bilingual review and a counterbalanced study of time versus error detection.” Link the evaluation rather than quoting fixture accuracy as a real-world result.
