# Example transcripts

These original, authored samples are generated from `src/data/demo.ts`. They are a fictional education episode, not actual YouTube output. Hindi and Tamil files intentionally contain changed quantities, lost negation and a terminology mismatch. They have not been independently bilingual-validated.

- Import `evidence.en.srt` as English and either `evidence.hi.srt` or `evidence.ta.srt` as the dub.
- The last English caption is split across two target captions to demonstrate pairing.
- The built-in sample also supplies a glossary term. If importing the raw files, add **random assignment** with Hindi variants **यादृच्छिक आवंटन | रैंडम असाइनमेंट**, or Tamil **சீரற்ற ஒதுக்கீடு**.
- No sample audio is provided. Attach your own matching media for a real review.

Expected demonstrations: 24→48 participants, a missing negation, terminology mismatch and ₹1,000→₹100. 60 seconds→one minute should not trigger a quantity flag. These examples are for exercising the workflow, not evaluating general translation quality.
